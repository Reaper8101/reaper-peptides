Compound Information

Educational information covering peptides, metabolic compounds, mitochondrial research and emerging areas of biomedical science.

Tirzepatide

A dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist studied extensively in metabolic and glucose-regulation research.

Retatrutide

Investigated for activity involving GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptor pathways in metabolic research.

KLOW

A peptide blend commonly discussed in relation to tissue repair, collagen and recovery research.

Epitalon

A synthetic peptide studied in aging biology, cellular regulation and telomere-related research.

SS-31

A mitochondria-targeted peptide investigated for mitochondrial function and cellular energy research.

NAD+

A naturally occurring coenzyme involved in cellular energy metabolism, redox reactions and DNA-associated processes.

MOTS-C

A mitochondria-derived peptide investigated for cellular metabolism, energy regulation and metabolic signaling.

Tesamorelin

A growth hormone-releasing hormone analog studied for its effects on growth-hormone signaling and body composition.

Ipamorelin

A growth hormone secretagogue investigated for its interaction with ghrelin receptors and growth-hormone release.

Pinealon

A short peptide studied primarily in experimental neuroscience, cellular regulation and aging-related research.
