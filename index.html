RBL // Peptide Basics

New to Peptides? Start Here.

Peptides are small chains of amino acids that researchers study because they can act as signals in the body. Different compounds are researched for different areas, including weight management, recovery, sleep, cellular energy, healthy aging and body composition.

01 // The Basics

What Are Peptides?

Amino acids are often called the building blocks of proteins. Peptides are simply shorter chains of those amino acids. Researchers study them because different peptides can interact with different processes in the body.

02 // Different Research Areas

Why Are There So Many?

Each compound has a different research focus. Some are studied mainly for appetite and metabolism, while others are researched for recovery, energy, sleep, healthy aging, brain health or muscle-related pathways.

03 // The Powder

Why Is It Freeze-Dried?

Many research peptides come as a dry powder instead of a liquid. This is called freeze-drying, or lyophilization. It is commonly used to help keep research material stable during storage and transportation.

04 // Keep It Simple

Don't Know the Science? That's Okay.

Names like MOTS-C, SS-31 and CJC-1295 can look complicated. You do not need to understand all of the chemistry to browse the research. Each compound card below gives you the simple version first, with more detail available if you want it.

Tirzepatide

Commonly researched for appetite control, weight management and blood-sugar regulation.

Weight Management • Appetite • Blood Sugar
Areas of Research Interest

Tirzepatide has been studied extensively for its effects on appetite regulation, glucose control, insulin sensitivity, body weight and waist circumference.

Research has also examined broader cardiometabolic measures including triglycerides, blood pressure, liver fat and metabolic-health markers.

Retatrutide

An experimental triple-action compound mainly researched for weight management, appetite and metabolism.

Weight Management • Appetite • Metabolism
Areas of Research Interest

Retatrutide activates GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptors, creating interest in how three metabolic signaling pathways may influence appetite, energy expenditure, glucose metabolism and body composition.

Clinical research has reported substantial dose-dependent changes in body weight, making it a closely watched next-generation metabolic research compound.

KLOW

A multi-peptide blend commonly researched for recovery, tissue support and collagen-related pathways.

Recovery • Tissue Support • Collagen
Areas of Research Interest

Research interest surrounding blends in this category generally centers on connective-tissue biology, collagen signaling, cellular repair mechanisms and recovery-related pathways.

Biological properties depend on the exact ingredients and concentrations in the specific formulation.

Epitalon

A short peptide mainly researched for healthy aging, longevity and cellular-aging pathways.

Longevity • Cellular Aging • Healthy Aging
Areas of Research Interest

Epitalon has been investigated for potential effects involving gene expression, cellular regulation, chromatin biology and mechanisms associated with aging.

Experimental research has also explored relationships with telomerase activity and telomere biology.

SS-31

A mitochondria-focused peptide researched for cellular energy, mitochondrial health and recovery.

Mitochondria • Cellular Energy • Recovery
Areas of Research Interest

SS-31, also known as elamipretide, is designed to localize within mitochondria and interact with cardiolipin in the inner mitochondrial membrane.

Research has focused on mitochondrial structure, ATP production, oxidative stress and cellular-energy efficiency.

NAD+

A naturally occurring molecule researched for cellular energy, metabolism and healthy-aging pathways.

Cellular Energy • Metabolism • Healthy Aging
Areas of Research Interest

NAD+ plays an essential role in redox reactions used by cells to generate energy and maintain metabolic function.

It also participates in signaling pathways involving DNA repair, sirtuin activity, cellular stress responses and mitochondrial maintenance.

MOTS-C

A mitochondria-derived peptide researched for metabolism, energy use and exercise-related adaptation.

Metabolism • Energy • Exercise Research
Areas of Research Interest

Research has explored relationships with glucose utilization, metabolic flexibility, skeletal-muscle function, cellular stress responses and exercise adaptation.

Experimental work has also examined mitochondrial efficiency and communication between mitochondria and the rest of the cell.

CJC-1295 Without DAC

A growth-hormone releasing hormone analog researched for GH signaling, recovery and body-composition pathways.

Growth Hormone • Recovery • Body Composition
Areas of Research Interest

CJC compounds are synthetic analogs of growth hormone-releasing hormone and are studied for their interaction with the growth-hormone signaling axis.

Research examines GH secretion, IGF-1 signaling, endocrine physiology, metabolic regulation and tissue-growth pathways.

Ipamorelin

A peptide researched for growth-hormone release, recovery and sleep-related pathways.

Growth Hormone • Recovery • Sleep Research
Areas of Research Interest

Ipamorelin interacts with the ghrelin receptor and has been studied for its ability to stimulate growth-hormone release.

Research areas associated with the GH axis include endocrine signaling, tissue recovery and metabolic regulation.

Pinealon

A short peptide mainly researched for brain health, cognitive aging and neuroprotection.

Brain Health • Cognitive Aging • Neuroprotection
Areas of Research Interest

Laboratory studies have examined relationships with oxidative stress, reactive oxygen species, cell viability and regulatory processes relevant to neuronal cells.

Evidence remains more limited and preclinical than that available for established metabolic therapies.

5-Amino-1MQ

A small-molecule compound mainly researched for metabolism, fat-loss pathways and body composition.

Metabolism • Fat-Loss Research • Body Composition
Areas of Research Interest

5-Amino-1MQ is investigated for how NNMT inhibition may influence cellular methylation balance, energy metabolism and metabolic signaling.

Published research remains developing, with much of the mechanistic work focused on preclinical models.

DSIP

A peptide mainly researched for sleep, relaxation and recovery-related pathways.

Sleep • Relaxation • Recovery Research
Areas of Research Interest

Delta sleep-inducing peptide has been studied in relation to sleep architecture, neuroendocrine signaling and physiological stress responses.

Evidence is limited and heterogeneous, so it remains primarily an experimental research peptide.

Somatotropin

Recombinant human growth hormone researched for growth, recovery and body-composition pathways.

Growth Hormone • Recovery • Body Composition
Areas of Research Interest

Somatotropin interacts with the growth-hormone receptor and influences downstream pathways including IGF-1 signaling, protein metabolism and tissue-growth biology.

Research spans endocrine physiology, body-composition biology and the broader growth-hormone signaling axis.

IGF-1 LR3

A long-acting IGF-1 analog mainly researched for muscle-growth pathways, recovery and cell growth.

Muscle-Growth Research • Recovery • Cell Growth
Areas of Research Interest

IGF-1 LR3 is engineered for prolonged activity relative to native IGF-1 and is used experimentally to study IGF-receptor signaling and downstream cellular pathways.

Research interest includes cell growth, nutrient signaling, glucose metabolism and tissue-development pathways.
