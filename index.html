Peptide Information

Learn about commonly discussed peptides and compounds, their areas of scientific interest, and ongoing research.

Retatrutide

Investigated for its activity involving GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptor pathways.

Tirzepatide

A dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist studied extensively in metabolic research.

KLOW

A peptide blend commonly discussed in relation to tissue, collagen and recovery research.

NAD+

A naturally occurring coenzyme involved in cellular energy metabolism and DNA-associated processes.

SS-31

Studied for interactions involving mitochondrial function and cellular energy systems.

MOTS-C

A mitochondria-derived peptide investigated for cellular metabolism and energy regulation.
