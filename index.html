RBL // Peptide Fundamentals

Understanding Peptides

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that act as signaling and regulatory molecules throughout biology. Because these small molecular messengers can interact with specific biological pathways, synthetic peptides have become an important area of biomedical, metabolic, mitochondrial and cellular research.

01 // Molecular Structure

What Are Peptides?

Peptides are composed of amino acids linked together by peptide bonds. They are structurally related to proteins but are generally shorter molecular chains. Naturally occurring peptides participate in numerous biological processes including cellular communication, metabolism, endocrine signaling and tissue regulation.

02 // Synthesis

How Research Peptides Are Manufactured

Synthetic peptides are commonly produced using controlled methods such as solid-phase peptide synthesis, where amino acids are assembled in a specific sequence. Following synthesis, compounds may undergo purification and analytical evaluation using techniques such as high-performance liquid chromatography and mass spectrometry to assess identity, composition and purity.

03 // Stabilization

Lyophilized Research Material

Many research peptides are supplied as a lyophilized, or freeze-dried, powder rather than as a liquid solution. Lyophilization removes water under controlled conditions and can improve stability during storage and transportation. A research vial may therefore contain only a small amount of visible dry material rather than liquid.

04 // Laboratory Handling

Laboratory Preparation

Depending on the compound and experimental protocol, lyophilized research material may require reconstitution with an appropriate sterile laboratory solvent before experimental use. Preparation methods, storage conditions, concentrations and routes used in published research vary considerably by compound and study design. Reaper BioLabs does not provide instructions for human administration, injection, dosing or self-treatment.

Tirzepatide

A dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist that has become a major focus of metabolic and cardiometabolic research.

Metabolism • Glucose Regulation • Body Composition
Areas of Research Interest

Tirzepatide has been studied extensively for its effects on appetite regulation, glucose control, insulin sensitivity, body weight and waist circumference.

Research has also examined changes in broader cardiometabolic measures including triglycerides, blood pressure, liver fat and markers associated with cardiovascular and metabolic health.

Its simultaneous activity at GIP and GLP-1 receptors has made tirzepatide important for studying how multiple metabolic pathways can influence energy balance and glucose regulation.

Retatrutide

An investigational triple-receptor agonist studied across several metabolic signaling pathways simultaneously.

Metabolism • Energy Balance • Body-Fat Research
Areas of Research Interest

Retatrutide activates GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptors, creating interest in how three complementary metabolic pathways may influence appetite, energy expenditure, glucose metabolism and body composition.

Clinical research has reported substantial dose-dependent changes in body weight, making retatrutide one of the most closely watched next-generation compounds in metabolic research.

Researchers are also exploring how glucagon receptor activity, when combined with incretin signaling, may affect energy balance and broader cardiometabolic physiology.

KLOW

A multi-peptide research blend commonly discussed in relation to tissue repair, collagen biology and recovery research.

Tissue Biology • Collagen • Recovery Research
Areas of Research Interest

KLOW is a blend designation rather than the name of a single standardized peptide molecule. Research interest surrounding blends in this category generally centers on connective-tissue biology, collagen signaling, cellular repair mechanisms and recovery-related pathways.

The biological properties of any peptide blend depend on its exact ingredients and concentrations, so conclusions should be based on the individual compounds within the formulation.

Epitalon

A synthetic tetrapeptide investigated in cellular-aging, longevity and gene-regulation research.

Cellular Aging • Telomeres • Longevity Biology
Areas of Research Interest

Epitalon has been investigated for potential effects on gene expression, cellular regulation, chromatin biology and mechanisms associated with biological aging.

Laboratory research has also explored relationships between Epitalon, telomerase activity and telomere biology.

SS-31

A mitochondria-targeted peptide investigated for cellular energy production and mitochondrial structure.

Mitochondria • ATP • Cellular Energy
Areas of Research Interest

SS-31, also known as elamipretide, is designed to localize within mitochondria and interact with cardiolipin in the inner mitochondrial membrane.

Research has focused on mitochondrial structure, ATP production, oxidative stress and cellular-energy efficiency.

NAD+

A naturally occurring cellular coenzyme central to energy metabolism and numerous regulatory processes.

Cellular Energy • Redox Biology • DNA Repair
Areas of Research Interest

NAD+ plays an essential role in redox reactions used by cells to generate energy and maintain normal metabolic function.

It also participates in signaling pathways involving DNA repair, sirtuin activity, cellular stress responses and mitochondrial maintenance.

MOTS-C

A mitochondria-derived peptide studied as a signaling molecule involved in cellular and metabolic regulation.

Metabolic Flexibility • Muscle • Mitochondrial Signaling
Areas of Research Interest

Research has explored its relationship with glucose utilization, metabolic flexibility, skeletal-muscle function, cellular stress responses and exercise adaptation.

Experimental work has also examined mitochondrial efficiency and oxidative-stress regulation.

CJC-1295 Without DAC

A GHRH analog investigated for its effects on growth-hormone signaling and endocrine physiology.

GH Signaling • IGF-1 Axis • Endocrine Research
Areas of Research Interest

CJC compounds are synthetic analogs of growth hormone-releasing hormone and are studied for their interaction with the body's growth-hormone signaling axis.

Research in this area examines GH secretion, IGF-1 signaling, endocrine physiology and metabolic regulation.

Ipamorelin

A selective growth-hormone secretagogue investigated for ghrelin-receptor and GH-axis signaling.

Growth Hormone • Ghrelin Receptor • Recovery Biology
Areas of Research Interest

Ipamorelin interacts with the ghrelin receptor and has been studied for its ability to stimulate growth-hormone release.

Research areas associated with the GH axis include endocrine signaling, body-composition biology, tissue recovery and metabolic regulation.

Pinealon

A short synthetic peptide investigated primarily in experimental neuroscience and cellular-aging research.

Neurobiology • Oxidative Stress • Cellular Aging
Areas of Research Interest

Laboratory studies have examined relationships with oxidative stress, reactive oxygen species, cell viability and several regulatory processes relevant to neuronal cells.

These findings have generated interest in Pinealon within neuroprotective and geroprotective research.
